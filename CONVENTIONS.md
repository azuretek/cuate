# Conventions

The rules this repository holds itself to live in [docs/CONTRIBUTING.md](docs/CONTRIBUTING.md). Most of them are enforced by a test in `core/test/guards.test.js`, so a change that breaks one fails CI rather than review.

Every design decision the app has made, with its reason, its issue and the test that holds it, is in [docs/CONVENTIONS.md](docs/CONVENTIONS.md).
