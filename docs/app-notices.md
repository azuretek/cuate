# App notices

App update, download and progress events use floating cards. Chat message notifications keep their existing native route and never enter this surface.

The reference is the sibling application's `core/ui/banner.js`, `core/ui/banner.css`, `core/spec/banner.json` and `core/spec/tokens.json` at commit `cc91effb65ac64dfd5ed9a55fb9b108569620781`. The card keeps its stroke tone icon, message, separate detail, native progress, action and dismiss control. Width (370px) and corners (12px) come from that source; palette, spacing and motion use this application's shared tokens. The tone glyphs (check, alert-triangle, circle-x, info) and the dismiss glyph (x) are the sibling's own, drawn from the shared icon set in `core/spec/tokens.json`, so the notice carries no SVG of its own. Arrival keeps the sibling banner's curve (`motion.normal` on `motion.ease`, as its `NoticeBanner.swift` arrival timing curve does); `motion.spring` is the sibling's first-screen arrival, not its notice arrival, so the notice does not take it.

Desktop cards enter from the right beneath the header and window controls. Phone-width cards enter from above in a band of their own at the top of the window, holding the safe-area insets, and every surface starts below that band, the open conversation drawer included, so a card never covers a control with the drawer open or closed; the band takes no room while it holds no card. The stack reserves the maximum composer height, is bounded by the dynamic viewport and does not claim transparent clicks. While a sheet is up (Settings, About, a confirm, the image viewer) the notices take a band of their own above it on every width, as a phone always does, in the backdrop's own dim: the sheet's backdrop is placed in the body, so it starts below the band. The answer to a press on a sheet (About's Check for updates, which runs the tray's own check) is then seen while the sheet stays up, and a notice never covers the sheet. Reduced motion disables arrival animation.

An operation has a stable identity and a revision naming its phase/version. Repeated events do nothing; progress changes the existing card. Dismiss marks that revision read without cancelling the operation. Further progress stays read; a ready, stalled or failed outcome can announce itself. No expiry timer steals an actionable notice. A transient card, the check in progress, stays up for `motion.min-visible` before a different state replaces it (the sibling's minimum-visible floor, as its `NoticeBoard.swift` applies to a transient answer); a standing state or a progress tick is never held, and a newer event replaces a held one rather than queueing behind it. The full-width update banner has been removed.

Verification: `core/test/app-notices.test.js` tests identity, progress, dismissal and geometry contracts. The supported desktop smoke exercises actual DOM identity, bridge actions, dismissal and captures synthetic light, dark and mobile layouts, the phone with the drawer open and closed; on the phone it fails when the card intersects any visible button, field or chat row, and names what it covered. Platform CI remains the release gate.
